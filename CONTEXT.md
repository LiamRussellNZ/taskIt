# TaskIt

TaskIt connects ASKERS who post tasks with DOERS who will perform or assist with them.

## Language

**Minimum Doer Rating**:
An optional eligibility threshold on a task. A doer with a rating must meet or exceed it; an unrated doer remains eligible.
_Avoid_: Task lock, rating range

**Primary Doer**:
The doer assigned to a claimed task.
_Avoid_: Assignee, claimant

**Helper**:
A secondary doer who offers assistance to the Primary Doer on a claimed task.
_Avoid_: Secondary doer, assistant

**Completion Review**:
A one-time 1–5 rating and optional written review that an asker gives the Primary Doer after task completion.
_Avoid_: Drop review, feedback
